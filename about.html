Our Story


  • ISL Pharmacy College (ISLPC) is the flagship program of AMES (The Ahmed Memorial Educational Society), representing the next chapter in our commitment to excellence in professional education.


  • Building upon the success of ISL Engineering College, established in 2008, ISLPC embodies the vision of providing world-class pharmaceutical education that meets international standards while remaining rooted in ISLPHARMACY COLLEGE our values of integrity, innovation, and service.


  • Located strategically in Hyderabad, the pharmaceutical capital of India, our college is positioned to leverage the city's rich ecosystem of pharmaceutical companies, research institutions, and healthcare organizations

    AMES Legacy



    The Ahmed Memorial Educational Society (AMES) has been a beacon of quality education since its inception. The ISLAMIA GROUP OF EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTIONS serves as the flagship organization of the society.

    Irrespective of region, religion, and caste, the society has ever been PHARMA engaged in imparting quality education. ISL Engineering College established in 2008, stands as a testament to our commitment to excellence in professional education.

    Under the visionary leadership of Mr. Khaja Hassan and the dynamic guidance of Mr. Salman Ahmed, our institutions have consistently delivered graduates who excel in their respective fields and contribute meaningfully to society.
